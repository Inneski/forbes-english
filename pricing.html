Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account page whenever you like — you'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

What's the difference between the plans?

All three plans include exactly the same access to every lesson on the site. The only difference is how often you're billed and the price — six-month and annual billing both work out to €7.50/month, a 17% saving over paying monthly.

Do I need an account?

Yes — you'll be asked to log in or create a free account before checkout, so we can link your subscription to your profile.
