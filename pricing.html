Questions

How does Block Camp work?

Term 1 is twelve missions. Mission 1 opens as soon as you pay, and a new mission opens every seven days after that. Once a mission opens it stays open for good: Term 1 is yours to keep, so a missed week is never lost. The six Block Camp specials come with it and are open from day one: three adventures (The Last Night at the Grand Hotel, Blocula, and the deep-sea Nautilus: The Black Archive) and three passive-voice decks (Present Perfect Passive, The Trial and Past Perfect Passive). Term 2 — the present perfect and past perfect lessons, passive stations 9 to 14, and the adventures The Long Way Home, Nautilus: The Black Archive and Twenty Thousand Leagues — is not part of Term 1; Forbes English Pro opens it. On a phone or tablet, add Block Camp to your Home Screen and it opens full screen, like an app.

Is anything a subscription?

Only Forbes English Pro. Block Camp, IELTS and essay marking are single payments that never renew. To cancel Forbes English Pro, email info@forbesenglish.com at any time; you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

What does Forbes English Pro include?

Every lesson on the site: the adult courses, IELTS, Block Camp and the whole Sherpa Tensing route, and every new lesson as it is published. Block Camp missions open one a week for subscribers too, counted from the first Block Camp lesson you open. Essay marking is not included; two marked essays are €49. You can also pay ahead: €45 every six months or €90 a year, both €7.50 a month, renewing until you cancel.

Who will I see on my receipt and bank statement?

Block Camp, IELTS and essay marking are sold through Link, Stripe’s checkout. Your receipt comes from Link, and your bank statement shows LINK.COM. Link also handles VAT on your purchase.

Who marks the IELTS essays?

Every essay is read and marked by a CELTA-qualified teacher — never by software. You get a band estimate and corrections within 5 working days.

What exactly is free?

Block Camp Mission 1, a lesson in every IELTS skill, the Sherpa Tensing route map and its first camps, and complete lessons at every level from A1 to C2. They are not trimmed or time-limited — you get the entire lesson, and you don't need an account to open them.

Do I need an account?

No. Press Buy and pay; your account is made from the email you give at checkout, and you are signed in as soon as you have paid. If that email already has an account, what you buy goes on it. You can set a password afterwards, on your account page.
