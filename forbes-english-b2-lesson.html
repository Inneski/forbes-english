📘 Grammar Focus: Four Key Structures

Reported Speech

Direct: "I love this car."
Reported: He said he loved that car.
Tense shifts back one step.

Mixed Conditionals

If + past perfect → would + infinitive
If he hadn't crashed, he would still be famous.
Past condition → present result.

Cleft Sentences

It was Jeremy who presented the show.
What I enjoy is the chemistry.
Used for emphasis and focus.

Complex Passives

He is said to be difficult.
The show is believed to have been cancelled.
Reporting verbs + passive infinitive.
