Before the questions

Formality is a whole clause, not a word

The verbs that carry it

A thing constitutes something rather than is it. A region is characterised by its features. A behaviour underpins what follows. A system serves as a channel for something.

Four verbs, and between them they carry most academic description.

What disqualifies an option

Vague quantifiers (wood and stone and stuff), discourse markers (basically, honestly), and the defining relative opened with is when. Each one is fine in speech and none survives in an essay.

A biome is when the land is different — the giveaway construction.

The trap

A formal word inside a casual frame is still casual: we do resource acquisition uses the phrase and gets it wrong, because the phrase needs no verb at all.

The whole clause has to change. That is what makes this hard.

The fixed pairings

Six collocations and their near-misses

Prepositions the verb fixes

You adhere to guidelines, and you comply with them — never comply to. Something is regarded as a thing or considered to be one, never viewed for it.

Capitalise on, not at. There is no rule; the verb simply takes one.

Connotation decides two of them

To exploit a mechanic is neutral and technical; misuse and abuse import a judgement the sentence is not making. To harness potential is positive, where exploit would insult the people described.

The same act, three attitudes. At C1 the attitude is the answer.

Terms a field has fixed

Content built to rules is procedurally generated. That is the industry’s own term; randomly constructed and computationally rendered describe nothing in particular and would not be understood.

When a field has a term, the near-synonym is not a stylistic choice.

The lexis

Words that only work in one grammar

Spawn is intransitive here

Mobs spawn; nobody spawns them. The word describes appearing under conditions, so a transitive use — the game spawns mobs in the sense of creates — misses what it means.

Venture forth is the same shape: intransitive, and it takes no object.

Iterative is not repetitive

An iterative process repeats in order to refine: each cycle improves on the last. Repetitive means the same thing again with no gain, which is the opposite claim about the work.

It comes from software, and it is now standard in any writing about design.

The abstract nouns

Vigilance is watchfulness maintained against a hazard. A conduit is a channel, usually metaphorical. Cultural resonance is reach and significance beyond the original field.

All three are formal, all three have plain equivalents, and the register is the point.

🗣Roleplay · in pairs

One of you is arguing that games belong in a curriculum; the other chairs the committee and is not persuaded. Four minutes each, then swap.

  • Define something from a game you know, in a sentence that would survive in an essay.
  • Make a claim, then hedge it, then evidence it — in that order and in three sentences.
  • Concede your opponent’s strongest point and then turn it, starting with despite.
  • Describe what a system does without once saying that it is anything.
✍️Writing · 200–250 words

Write the opening of a paper arguing that a game deserves academic attention. Define your object, concede the obvious objection, make one claim and evidence it, and close by saying what the field currently holds. Hedge what deserves hedging and let no clause slip into speech.
