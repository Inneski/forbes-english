# Talk Me Through the Last Job: Midjourney brief

Generated by `py lesson-template/build/build_siteinterviews.py --brief`; edit PLATES in the builder, not this file.

The deck shipped on plates borrowed from Within Spec, Below Standard, Talking About Your Product and the roofing lesson. These 22 are its own. Each slide switches to its own picture the moment that picture is in, one at a time, so there is no need to make them all at once.

## How to use it

1. Paste each prompt into Midjourney as it is. `--sref` points at the roofers plate already on the live site, so the new set matches the borrowed one; add your own style images as usual.
2. Pick the best of the four, upscale it, and save it under the name shown (`hero.png`, `p-warm.png` …) in
   [C:\Users\black\Documents\FORBES\incoming\site-interviews](../incoming/site-interviews)
3. Tell a local Claude session "site interviews art is in", or run `/publish`.

Keep the subject in the middle with calm space round it: the deck crops to the subject, and the cover cuts the hero out and pops it out of a square.
