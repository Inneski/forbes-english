card-art map in `library.html`, and the artwork folders on disk. Method, so it
can be regenerated rather than trusted:

## Merge before commissioning

Three pairs are the *same lesson twice*. Commissioning art for both halves is
paying twice for one hero.

### Confirmed identical — merge now

**`forbes-english-meetings.html` ⇄ `forbes-english-ope- say in your words.html`**
238 item strings shared, **zero unique to either side**. The same lesson, filed
twice. `forbes-english-meetings.html` is the deck (`deck: true`, built by
`build_meetings.py`); the other is the pre-rebuild original that was never
retired. Retire `forbes-english-ope- say in your words.html`. Both are `pro`,
both are in the catalogue, so a learner can currently pay for the same content
under two titles.

**`-dinosaurs C1.html` ⇄ `forbes-english-lesson (dinosoausrs c1).html`**

`forbes-english-dinosaur-minecraft.html` overlaps both at 0.80–0.88 — a
three-file cluster that should be one lesson, or one lesson plus a genuinely
distinct Minecraft edition.

**`forbes-english-football-b1.html` ⇄ `…-argentina.html`**

### Same lesson, different support language — mergeable, but blocked

**`full_grammar_test.html` (DE) ⇄ `full_grammar_test_italian.html` (IT)** — **DONE**
**1242 of 1253 strings identical.** The only differences are eleven pieces of
page chrome — title, section headings, button labels, "Dein Ergebnis" against
"Il tuo risultato". And both files **already ship the same nine-language
switcher** (ar, de, es, fr, it, ja, pt, ru, zh). Two files that differ only in
which language the chrome is hardcoded to, when the switcher is right there.
This one is a clean merge with no content loss, and it has been made: one
file, starting language from `?lang=`, the Italian filename kept as a redirect
to `?lang=it` so bookmarks survive, and the duplicate catalogue row removed.
English was added to the switcher at the same time — `UI_I18N` and
`SECTION_GLOSS` always had an `en` entry that nothing could reach.

**`forbes-english-body-parts-c1.html` ⇄ `…-c1-zh.html`**
156 shared. The differences are the gloss field alone: `de:` against `zh:`,
"a German equivalent" against "a Chinese equivalent". Same items, one data
column swapped. Merge into one lesson with a language-selectable gloss.

**`cheat_sheet.html` (DE) ⇄ `cheat_sheet_italian.html` (IT)**
149 shared, 39 unique each — and the unique strings are the *example sentences
and explanations*, in German against Italian. No switcher in either.

> **The blocker.** Merging any lesson whose L1 support is per-item, rather than
> chrome, currently destroys the thing that makes the second file worth having.
> `UI_I18N` resolves `data-i18n` keys only; per-item feedback lives in
> `data-explain`, which the switcher never reaches. Until that is fixed
> (recorded in `HANDOFF.md`), `full_grammar_test` and `body-parts` can merge
> safely — their support is chrome and a gloss field respectively — and
> `cheat_sheet` cannot.
