# Grammar page: improvement plan

Innes, 2026-11-20: "create a plan for improving this page". The page is
forbesenglish.com/grammar (`grammar.html`). It is generated by
`tools/build_grammar_hub.py`, with the chart rack from `tools/grammar_maps.py`
and the topics from `tools/topics.py`; never edit `grammar.html` by hand.

## What it is now (measured live, 2026-11-20)

| | 1920 × 1080 | Phone (Pixel 7) |
|---|---|---|
| Page length | 9,036 px = 8.4 screens | 18.6 screens |
| Topic shelves start at | screen 3.9 | screen 6.4 |
| Topic shelves take | 3,147 px | 9.7 screens |
| Grammar maps take | 1,802 px | 2.8 screens |
| First-load weight | 2.0 MB, 51 requests (images 1.6 MB; three.js 196 KB; hero.jpg 543 KB) | same, plus a WebGL canvas |
| Load event | 3.4 s | 0.9 s |

The order is:

1. hero: the 3D relief of the Gerundia chart;
2. Grammar maps: a rack of 25 rolls beside one open chart;
3. Tense code: twelve coloured squares, plus going to;
4. Shelves: 19 topic cards in three groups (Tenses, Grammar, Skills);
5. Two routes: Sherpa Tensing and Block Camp;
6. two notes;
7. the Level Check close.

The page has no footer, and nor do index, library, ielts or block-camp.

### What works

- The tense code. It is the clearest thing on the page, and it ties every lesson to the Sherpa colours.
- The rack idea, and the charts themselves.
- The counts in the hero (345 lessons, 19 topics, 90 free) and the "free first" promise.

### What does not

1. **The page answers its own headline late.** The H1 is "English grammar, by
   topic", but the topics start 3.9 screens down on a desktop and 6.4 on a
   phone. Before them come the maps, whose names are places rather than grammar
   points, and the tense code. A learner who arrives from Google for "passive
   voice" has to scroll past 25 fanciful names to find it.
2. **The rack is a list in the order the charts were built.**
   - The tags name places (*The Bridges*, *Statia & Actia*, *The Backshift
     Current*), not grammar points. Only the open sheet says what a chart
     teaches.
   - One tag is cut short: "The -ER Coast & the MORE…".
   - At 1920 the open chart and its words fill about 450 px, while the rack
     runs about 1,150 px. That leaves roughly 700 px of empty column beside the
     lower rolls.
3. **The hero copy sits on busy lettering.** Map names ("Try Tarn", "Remember
   Rock", "Go-On Gill") show through the paragraph and the stats. The halo
   softens this but does not remove it.
4. **The hero is heavy for what it does.**
   - three.js and a 543 KB JPEG load on every visit, phones included, for a
     relief the reader scrolls past.
   - The whole page is 2.0 MB before anyone taps anything.
5. **Two topic cards share a picture with another card.** Future Tenses has
   the same image as Tense Review and Grammar Tests, and Conditionals the same
   as Business English (`grammar-hub/*-1b321f2e.jpg`, `*-17f226f3.jpg`).
6. **The shelf pictures are four styles at once:** voxel game art, pixel
   posters, film-poster stills and a diagram screenshot (Used To). On a phone
   each card is a full-width picture, which is most of the 9.7 screens.
7. **"Skills" is not grammar.** Business English, IELTS Academic and
   Vocabulary (186 lessons) make the grammar page longer without serving its
   reader.
8. **One thing appears twice.** Tense review shows as a card on the Tenses
   shelf and again as the black "All of them at once" bar under the tense code.
9. **No way to search.** The page claims 345 lessons but offers no filter; the
   library's filters are only linked at the very bottom.
10. **On a phone the top bar shows only Library, the language and Go Pro.**
    Check that RPGs, Level Check and the rest are reachable from it on this page.

## The plan

The phases are in order of value per hour. Each ends in something shippable.

### Phase 1: lead with the promise: DONE 2026-11-20

What shipped, and where it differs from the plan below:

- **The topic index** (`find_band()` in `tools/build_grammar_hub.py`) sits
  under the hero: 19 chips in three rows. It starts 0.85 screens down at 1920
  and is 582 px tall; on a phone it starts at 1.2 screens and is 1.4 screens
  tall.
- **The filter searches the charts too.** Matching charts appear as a fourth
  row of chips inside the index, rather than narrowing the rack two sections
  down, where the reader would not see it. Results: "passive" gives Passive
  Voice, the Passive Ferry and the Sherpa route map; "in on at" gives
  Prepositions and the Harbour of Scale. Enter opens the first match;
  Escape clears. The words each topic answers to are `FIND_WORDS`.
- **The section order** is hero, index, tense code, maps, shelves, routes,
  close. The hero's first button is "Find your grammar point"; the close's
  second is "Back to the topics".
- **Tense review** is no longer doubled: the black "All of them at once" bar
  under the tense code is gone, and its line under the squares links to Tense
  Review instead.
- **No two cards share a picture.** `card_picture()` picks the next lesson's
  picture, and the build stops if none is left. The comparison is by the
  file's bytes.
- **The -ER & MORE roll** has `short` in `grammar_maps.py`. All 25 roll
  names were measured uncut at 1920 and on a phone.
- **The phone bar, site-wide.** On a phone every page but the front page
  showed only Library, the language and Go Pro. `fe-lang.js` now turns
  Library into a menu of every link in the row whenever any link is out of
  it, as the front page does. It works on every page that loads the script:
  the hubs, IELTS, the library, pricing, account, RPG, the Block Camp pages
  and the grammar page. At 1280 the menu stands where Library stands, which
  also makes Slide Decks and Work With Me reachable there. Block Camp keeps
  its own language menu and gets only the site menu. Its RPGs-first squeeze
  order now matches the front page's.

### Phase 2: make the rack a guide: DONE 2026-11-21

What shipped, and where it differs from the plan below:

- **Shelves.** `SHELVES` at the foot of `tools/grammar_maps.py` puts the 25
  charts on nine shelves, in teaching order, each under a heading: Tenses,
  Verb forms, Modals and certainty, Phrasal verbs and collocations, Nouns and
  articles, Adjectives and adverbs, Prepositions, Sentences, Words and
  register. The build stops if a chart is on no shelf or on two. At 1920 a
  heading takes its own row in the rack's column, on the page's paper, so the
  uprights stop and start again: one rack a shelf. Regularia, the first chart
  on the first shelf, is out when the page opens.
- **Every roll names its grammar point** on a second line (`chart_point()`,
  from the chart's heading): "The Bridges / Linking words". No name or point
  is cut, measured at 1920 and on a phone.
- **Under the open chart** (`more_for()`):
  - A chart with a real topic shows "Practise it": three lessons on that
    point, free first, then "All N lessons on <topic>".
  - A chart filed only under a catch-all (`CATCH_ALL`: Vocabulary, Tense
    Review, Business, IELTS) shows the other charts on its shelf instead.
    For those, "lessons on the same topic" would be unrelated.

  The sheet was already sticky; this fills the column beside the rack.
- **Topic pages link their charts** (`map_links()` in `tools/build_hubs.py`):
  an "On the grammar map" block under the facts, beside the intro. Up to
  three charts appear as cards with the chart's picture (the copy
  grammar.html already makes); more than three appear as one line pointing
  to the rack (Vocabulary has 7, Tense Review 4). The chart sheets link their
  topic page through "All N lessons on …".

### Phase 2: make the rack a guide (the brief it was built from)

6. **Group the rolls by grammar area,** with a small heading over each group:
   - Tenses and time: Regularia, Finished & Open Time, Statia & Actia, the Passive Ferry, the Backshift Current, the Conditional Quadrants, Sherpa Tensing.
   - Verb patterns: Gerundia, the Particle Ferries, Makeland & Doland.

   - Sentences: the Return Voyage, the Inversion Maelstrom, the Bridges.
   - Words and register: the Prefix Archipelago, the Heights of Register, the Atlantic.

   The groups live in `grammar_maps.py`, as a field on each map.
7. **Each tag names its grammar point** under the place name ("The Bridges ·

8. **Use the empty column at 1920.**
   - The open chart becomes sticky, staying beside the rack as the reader moves

   - Under it go two or three lessons from the same topic, free ones first.
9. **Cross-link both ways:** each topic page links its chart or charts, and each
   chart's sheet links its topic page.

### Phase 3: a lighter, more legible hero: DONE 2026-11-21

What shipped (measured on a local build, first screen, no scrolling,
decompressed bytes):

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Phone | 2.84 MB: three.js 1.2 MB, the 543 KB JPEG fetched twice | **690 KB**: the 175 KB WebP, no three.js, the still chart at once |
| Desktop, 3D | 3.0 MB, load event 1.44 s | three.js after the load event; the 330 KB WebP; load event 0.95 s locally (the live figure is in HANDOFF) |
| Reduced motion | 3D ran anyway | the still chart, no three.js |

- **The panel.** At 881 px and up, the copy sits on parchment: the card
  colour at 85% with a 6 px blur, as on the chart pages. The wash across the
  left of the map and the pale halo on every line are gone, so the map keeps
  its colour around the panel. Contrast is measured in `CONTRAST`: ink on
  card 15.1:1.
- **Who gets the 3D.** One check in the hero (`window.GH_3D`): at least 881 px
  wide, no `prefers-reduced-motion`, no Save-Data. Only then is `gh-wait`
  set (the page opening on the sky), and only then, after the load event,
  does the module run. It sits in the page as inert text (`#gh-3d-src`) until
  the loader turns it into a module script.
- **WebP.** `web_webp()` and `hero_picture()` make 1536 px and 1080 px WebP
  copies at quality 72. Compared at double size with the JPEG on the
  Shallows lettering, there is no visible difference. They serve both the
  hero and the closing band through `<picture>`, with the JPEG for older
  browsers. `sizes` says 94vw under 880 px, so a 412 px phone at 2.625x
  takes the 1080 copy, not the 1536. `prune()` now clears stale `.webp` as
  well as `.jpg`.
- **Tried and dropped:** handing the decoded `<img>` straight to WebGL, to
  avoid the 3D loader's second request. It uploaded black (a grey relief with
  no chart on it), the failure the old comment warned about. The loader
  stays. The `<img>` is `crossorigin="anonymous"` like the loader, so where
  caching is allowed the second request is served from the browser's copy.

### Phase 3: a lighter, more legible hero (the brief it was built from)

## How to check each phase

Measure live at 1920 and on a phone (Pixel 7), with
`scratchpad/audit.js`-style captures:

- page length in screens;
- where the topic index starts;
- first-load KB;
- no duplicate pictures;
- every chip, roll and card hit by `elementFromPoint`.

Then run `py tools/build_hubs.py`, then `py tools/seo.py`, and push. Check that
GitHub's check runs show a "Workers Builds: forbes-english" run; a Pages
success alone does not update the live site.
