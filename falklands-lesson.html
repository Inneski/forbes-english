The Falklands Dispute: Sovereignty, Self-Determination & Diplomacy

A BBC News report raised fresh questions about US policy on the Falkland Islands — a British overseas territory claimed by Argentina — after an internal Pentagon email suggested Washington may reconsider its position. Downing Street immediately reaffirmed that sovereignty rests with the UK and that the islanders' right to self-determination is paramount.

The islands lie in the south-west Atlantic Ocean. A ten-week war was fought over them in 1982. In a 2013 referendum, all but three of the 1,650 eligible voters chose to remain British, on a turnout of over 90%.
