# Block Camp II — Passive Voice Descent

A standalone companion route map for the far side of Block Camp. Part I climbs from base camp to the lookout through eight tense stations; Part II begins at the watchtower and descends through eight passive-voice stations.

## Route

9. Active → Passive
10. BE + Past Participle
11. Present Simple Passive
12. Past Simple Passive
13. Continuous Passive
14. Present Perfect Passive
15. Future + Modal Passive
16. The Passive Voice Trial

The sequence deliberately teaches the concept and core form before tense-specific variants. The final station covers agent choice and when to omit `by + agent`.

## Visual continuity

- Uses the first-light watchtower view from the end of Part I.
- The path begins at the tower railing and descends toward the distant pass.
- Natural pine-and-birch colours remain unchanged.
- Cream route line and lantern-orange progress match Part I.
- Monocraft Black is embedded for the main header.
- No characters, mobs, logos, text or proprietary game assets are baked into the artwork.

## Integration

Open `index.html` directly or host the folder unchanged. The preview's **Complete station** button simulates lesson completion.

The real hub can update progress with:

```js
BlockCampPassiveProgress.setCompleted(1); // through 8
```

Progress is forward-only during the session. Add `?completed=3` to the URL to open the proof at a particular state.

The Part I link currently points to:

`https://forbesenglish.com/block-camp-map`
