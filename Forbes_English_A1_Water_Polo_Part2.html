Part 2 is more difficult. You will practise harder water polo words and learn to build full questions correctly.

Remember the pattern: question word + auxiliary (do/does/is/are) + subject + verb

Example: Where does the goalkeeper stand?  |  Which team won the match?

New question word: Which = Welche(r/s) – we use it when there is a choice between specific options.

Free Speaking Round

Now You Talk!

No right or wrong answers here — just speak! Try to use full questions and the new words from this lesson. Talk to your teacher or partner about these questions.

1
Why do you think exclusions are important in water polo? Warum sind Ausschlüsse im Wasserball deiner Meinung nach wichtig?
2
Which position would you rather play: goalkeeper or attacker? Why? Welche Position würdest du lieber spielen: Torwart oder Angreifer? Warum?
3
How do you think a team wins possession of the ball? Wie gewinnt eine Mannschaft deiner Meinung nach den Ballbesitz?
4
Ask your partner three questions about their favourite sport, using question words from this lesson. Stelle deinem Partner drei Fragen zu seiner Lieblingssportart, benutze dabei Fragewörter aus dieser Lektion.
5
Describe what is happening in the picture at the top of this page. Beschreibe, was auf dem Bild oben auf dieser Seite passiert.
