Holmes’s language lab · concept check

Say only what the evidence supports.

Past deduction
must have + PP — almost certain
might / could have + PP — possible
can’t have + PP — impossible

Reported speech
“I saw her.” → He said he had seen her.
“I will return.” → He said he would return.
told needs a listener: He told Watson…

Answer aloud: when I say must have, did I see it happen? Am I nearly certain? In had seen, which happened first?

The complete solution

Two crimes overlapped.

The theft
Celeste stole a decoy at Sir Edmund’s own request. The real Star had already gone to Baker Street inside the sealed tea tin.

The murder
During his 6:10 visit Moriarty poisoned the gift tobacco. Sir Edmund smoked it later and died at 9:12 — so the alibi was genuine.

The staging framed Celeste and invented an impossible intruder. The Star itself was an optical cipher naming Moriarty’s network.
