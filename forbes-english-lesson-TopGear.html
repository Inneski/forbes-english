📖 Quick Reference: Active vs. Passive Voice

In the active voice, the subject performs the action. In the passive voice, the subject receives the action. We form the passive with be + past participle.

Active

Jeremy Clarkson drove the Ferrari.
(Subject → Verb → Object)

Passive

The Ferrari was driven by Jeremy Clarkson.
(Object becomes subject)

Active – Present

The producers film the show in the UK.

Passive – Present

The show is filmed in the UK.

Describe a Top Gear moment

Think of a car, a challenge, or a stunt from a show like Top Gear (real or invented). Write 3–4 sentences describing it — use both active and passive voice at least once each. For example: "The presenters drove three classic cars across the desert. The route was chosen by the producers to test the cars' reliability."
