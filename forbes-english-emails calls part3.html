The shape of it

Four moves, in this order, every time

1 · Open

Warm, brief, no news: I hope this email finds you well.

2 · State the purpose

Immediately, in one line: I am writing to follow up on…

3 · Make the ask

Specific, and easy to say yes to: Would Wednesday afternoon suit you?

4 · Close with a commitment

Who does what, by when: I will send a recap by end of day.

Register

The same message at three temperatures

Too cold

“I sent the proposal three days ago and am still waiting.”

Counts the days. Puts the reader on the back foot.

About right

“I wanted to check whether you have had a chance to review it.”

Offers them a reason for the silence. Still asks.

Too warm

“Sorry to bother you again, I know how busy you must be!”

Apologises for existing. Buries the ask.

🗣Discussion · in pairs

Run these as calls, not conversations. One of you is on a bad line.

  • Ask a client to repeat a figure you did not catch — twice, without saying “what?”
  • End the call by confirming who does what and by when. Your partner must be able to repeat it back.
  • Now the client is annoyed the report was late. Open your reply without defending yourself.
