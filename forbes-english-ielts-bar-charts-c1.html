Mastering the Bar Chart Report

Learn the four-paragraph structure examiners are trained to look for — and practise the paraphrasing, comparatives and linking language that separate a band 6 report from a band 9.

The report, in four paragraphs

Every strong Task 1 bar chart report follows the same shape. Skim this before you start — you'll be tested on it.

Paragraph 1

Introduction

Paraphrase the exam prompt. Keep words like "chart" or "graph" unchanged — not everything can be reworded — but swap verbs and nouns where you can, and state the units.

Paragraph 2 · worth the most

Overview

Without this paragraph your Task Achievement score is capped at band 5. State 2–4 key features only — the highest, the lowest, the overall comparison — no small data points.

Paragraph 3

Body A

Group categories logically — by extremes (highest/lowest) or by trend (which country spent more). Support every comparative with real figures.

Paragraph 4

Body B

Cover the remaining categories using the same logic as Body A. Two body paragraphs are required — this is marked under Coherence & Cohesion.
