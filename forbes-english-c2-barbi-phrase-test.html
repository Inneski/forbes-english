PART 4 Two new arrivals

Two phrases outside the dossiers. Build your own sentence for each, set in one of the six COO scenes, before checking the note.

New phrase

redeeming qualities — positive traits that make up for someone's or something's flaws.

Your sentence

Try it about the finalist who ghosted the client, or the manager who resists change — what redeems them, despite the problem?

"He was impossible to manage, but his redeeming quality was that clients trusted him instantly — that's worth protecting."
New phrase

to skedaddle — to leave quickly, often to escape an awkward or dangerous situation. Informal, but good for a knowing, self-aware register even at C2.

Your sentence

Try it about ending the partnership dinner before the exclusivity demand gets awkward, or leaving a meeting the moment the numbers turn ugly.

"The moment he reached for the contract at dessert, I decided it was time to skedaddle before I promised something I couldn't deliver."
