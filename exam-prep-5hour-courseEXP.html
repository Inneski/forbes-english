⏱️ Hour 2

Present Simple + Present Progressive

Two present tenses. One is for habits and facts. One is for now.

Present Simple — habits & facts

PositiveNegativeQuestion
I play tennis.I don't play tennis.Do you play tennis?
She plays tennis.She doesn't play tennis.Does she play tennis?
Signal words: usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays.

PositiveNegativeQuestion

one or ones?

Use one (singular) or ones (plural) so you don't repeat the noun.

Instead of saying…Say…
I like the red jacket.I like the red one.
I want the blue shoes.I want the blue ones.

Reading skills

Before you answer: 1. Read the title. 2. Look at pictures. 3. Underline names, times, places, numbers. 4. Read the question. 5. Find the answer. 6. Write a full sentence if asked.
Yesterday, Mia went to a restaurant with her family. She wanted pizza, but there wasn't any pizza left. Her brother had chicken and potatoes. Mia had pancakes with strawberries. Later, they bought some ice cream.

Guided Writing + Mock Test

Put it all together: write a 100–120 word text, then take the mock test.

Story structure

PartQuestion to answer
BeginningWhen? Who? Where?
MiddleWhat happened?
ProblemWhat went wrong?
EndingWhat happened in the end?
FunctionUseful phrases
BeginningLast weekend, One day, Yesterday
SequenceFirst, Then, After that, Later
SurpriseSuddenly, But then, Unfortunately
EndingIn the end, Finally, Luckily

Model story (~110 words)

Last Saturday, Mia and her brother went to the space centre. They looked at a big spaceship and took some photos. Suddenly, they heard a strange noise. An alien came out of the spaceship! At first, Mia was scared, but the alien was friendly. He was hungry, so they gave him some pancakes and strawberries. He didn't like the pancakes, but he loved the strawberries. Later, the alien went back into the spaceship. Before he left, he gave Mia a small green stone. In the end, Mia was happy because she had an exciting story to tell at school.

Model restaurant dialogue

Waiter: Good evening. What would you like?
Girl: I'd like a pizza, please.
Waiter: Which one would you like?
Girl: The large one with tomatoes and peppers.
Waiter: Would you like some salad?
Girl: Yes, please. Have you got any orange juice?
Waiter: No, sorry, but we have some apple juice.
Girl: That's fine. I'll have the apple juice.
