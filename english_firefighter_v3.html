MUST & HAVE TO

🇬🇧 English for German Firefighters · All Tenses · B1–B2

In this lesson you will review how must and have to work across every tense — present, past, future, past regrets and conditional — with sentences from real firefighting situations. Use the buttons below to move through the slides.

Grammar reference
must vs. have to

🔴 must

🇩🇪 müssen — innere Überzeugung, Befehl, Verbot
UseExample
PresentYou must wear your helmet.innere Überzeugung / Regel
FutureI must train next week.persönlicher Entschluss
ForbiddenYou must not enter.darf nicht = Verbot!
Past ⚠️No past form! → use had tomüssen (Vergangenheit) = had to

🔵 have to

🇩🇪 müssen — äußere Pflicht, Vorschriften, Regeln
TenseExample
PresentI have to check the hose.externe Regel
PastWe had to evacuate.mussten → had to
PerfectHe has had to report.hat … müssen → has had to
FutureShe will have to sign.wird … müssen → will have to
Not nec.don't have to = not neededbraucht nicht ≠ must not!

⚠️ Achtung! Common mistakes for German speakers

❌ Wrong
  • "You must not come" (= forbidden!)
    when you mean: not necessary
  • "I must go yesterday"
    must has NO past tense!
✅ Correct
  • "You don't have to come" = nicht nötig
  • "I had to go yesterday" = ich musste
⏪ Past – had to / has had to 🇩🇪 Vergangenheit
1
Gestern mussten wir sehr schnell reagieren.
Yesterday, we ________ react very quickly. Simple Past
2
Der Feuerwehrmann musste die Leiter aufstellen.
The firefighter ________ put up the ladder. Simple Past
3
Er hat diese Woche schon zweimal Überstunden machen müssen.
He ________ do overtime twice this week already. Present Perfect
⏺ Present – must / have to / must not / don't have to 🇩🇪 Gegenwart
4
Es gibt eine offizielle Vorschrift: Alle müssen einen Helm tragen.
According to regulations, all firefighters ________ wear a helmet on site. Present – external rule
5
Ich persönlich bin überzeugt: Das ist wirklich sehr gefährlich!
I think this building is really dangerous — everyone ________ leave now! Present – strong conviction
6
Du darfst auf keinen Fall das Gebäude betreten — Lebensgefahr!
You ________ enter that building — it is life-threatening! Present – forbidden
7
Es ist kein Pflichttermin — er braucht nicht zu kommen.
It is optional — he ________ attend the meeting. Present – not necessary
⏩ Future – must / will have to 🇩🇪 Zukunft
8
Ich habe mir fest vorgenommen: Nächste Woche gehe ich ins Fitnessstudio.
I ________ go to the gym next week — I've already decided. Future – personal decision
9
Die neue Vorschrift tritt nächsten Monat in Kraft — alle werden sich daran halten müssen.
The new regulation starts next month — everyone ________ follow it. Future – external obligation
10
Nach dem Einsatz wird der Kommandant einen Bericht erstatten müssen.
After the operation, the commander ________ submit a full report. Future – external rule
😔 Past Regrets & Criticism – should have / shouldn't have / needn't have 🇩🇪 Vorwürfe & Bedauern

How it works

subject+ should have / shouldn't have / needn't have+ past participle

Should have + past participle talks about a past action that didn't happen but would have been the right thing to do — a regret or a criticism.

  • should have — something good that didn't happen: You should have raised the alarm earlier. (but you didn't)
  • shouldn't have — something bad that did happen: He shouldn't have entered that building. (but he did)
  • needn't have — something unnecessary that was done anyway: You needn't have stayed all night. (you did, but it wasn't necessary)

🇩🇪 Entspricht hätte(n) … sollen im Deutschen — ein Bedauern oder ein Vorwurf über die Vergangenheit.

11
Du hättest früher Alarm schlagen sollen! (Vorwurf)
You ________ raised the alarm earlier! We lost time. Criticism
12
Wir hätten mehr trainieren sollen — der Einsatz war sehr schwierig.
We ________ trained more — the operation was very difficult. Regret
13
Er hat das Gebäude betreten — das war falsch und gefährlich.
He ________ entered that building — it was far too dangerous. Criticism – wrong action
14
Es war doch gar nicht nötig, dass du die ganze Nacht geblieben bist!
You ________ stayed all night — the situation was under control! Unnecessary past action
15
Der Einsatzleiter bedauert: Ich hätte die Entscheidung anders treffen sollen.
The incident commander says: "I ________ made a different decision — I regret it now." Personal regret
💭 Conditional – would have to 🇩🇪 Konjunktiv II
16
Wenn der Brand größer wäre, müsstest du alle evakuieren.
If the fire were bigger, you ________ evacuate the whole area. Conditional
17
Ohne das neue Fahrzeug müssten wir viel länger warten.
Without the new vehicle, we ________ wait much longer. Conditional
