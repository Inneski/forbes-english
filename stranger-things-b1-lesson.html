GRAMMAR
TENSES · VERB PATTERNS · GERMAN TRAPS
Reference — Study these
PRESENT SIMPLE PRESENT SIMPLE — facts that are always true
✔ My cat jumps onto the door handle and opens the door.
→ This is a fact / a habit. It happens regularly.
PAST SIMPLE PAST SIMPLE — a finished action
✔ He learned it by watching what we do.
→ The learning happened once in the past. It is finished.
PRESENT PERFECT vs. PAST SIMPLE PRESENT PERFECT
✔ I have never seen the film V.   ← experience up to now
✗ I didn't see the film V. ← only correct if you specify a time: "I didn't see it yesterday."

✔ We haven't spoken since our last argument.
✗ We don't spoke since our last argument. ← NICHT auf Deutsch übersetzen!
German Traps ⚡ — Don't translate directly!
DO homework — NICHT "make"
✔ We have to do our homework, otherwise we'll be in trouble.
✗ We have to make our homework.
GET a message — NICHT "become"
✔ I got a message from Will last night.
✗ I became a message from Will.
STUDY / LEARN — NICHT "lernen" directly
✔ I had to study for the test.
✗ I had to learn for the test.

✔ If I don't study, I will get a bad grade.
✔ I will have to study hard to get a good grade.
VERB PATTERNS — to rely on / to be good at
✔ Eleven can rely on her friends. [rely on + somebody]
✔ Dustin is good at science. [be good at + noun / -ing]
✔ Mike is good at making plans.

READING
THE WORLD OF STRANGER THINGS
Read — then answer the questions below

Stranger Things is a popular American science fiction horror series set in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, during the 1980s. It was created by Matt and Ross Duffer, known as the Duffer Brothers, and first appeared on Netflix in 2016. The show relies on nostalgia for 1980s popular culture, from the music to the fashion and even the fonts used in its title sequence.


The story begins when a boy named Will Byers goes missing in Hawkins. His friends — Mike, Dustin, and Lucas — set out to find him. During their search, they encounter a strange girl who has escaped from a secret government laboratory. She is known only as Eleven. She was taught to use her powerful psychokinetic abilities by scientists in the laboratory, who treated her as a test subject rather than a human being.


The laboratory is connected to a dark, terrifying parallel dimension called the Upside Down. In this world, certain creatures — particularly a monster called the Demogorgon — pose a severe danger to anyone they encounter. Eleven avoided capture for years and eventually formed a bond with the group of friends, learning to trust and rely on them. The series explores themes of friendship, courage, the stigma around being different, and the dangers of government secrecy.
