Active & Passive Voice

Before you begin the activities, read this short grammar reference. You can scroll back to it any time during the lesson.

▶ Active Voice

Subject → Verb → Object

The subject performs the action. This is the most common voice in English. It is direct and clear.

🧱 Lego releases new sets every year.

▶ Passive Voice

Subject + be + past participle

The subject receives the action. The agent (the doer) may be omitted or introduced with by.

🧱 New sets are released every year (by Lego).

🔄 How to Transform Active → Passive

ActiveOle Kirk Christiansen founded Lego in 1932.
Object → becomes new Subject  |  Verb → be + past participle  |  Subject → by + agent (optional)
PassiveLego was founded by Ole Kirk Christiansen in 1932.
TenseActivePassive Form
Present SimpleLego makes bricks.is/are + made
Past SimpleThey built a castle.was/were + built
Present PerfectShe has tested the set.has/have + been + tested
Past PerfectHe had launched it.had + been + launched
Modal (will/can…)You can assemble it.can + be + assembled
Passive Infinitive—to be + past participle
🕒 When to use Passive

• The doer is unknown: The brick was dropped.
• The doer is obvious or unimportant
• To focus on the result, not the person
• In formal / scientific writing

⚠ Common Mistakes

• Using was been instead of was / has been
• Wrong agreement: The bricks was made ✗
• Forgetting by when the agent matters
• Using wrong past participle form
