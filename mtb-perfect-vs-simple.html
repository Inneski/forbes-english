Present Perfect
vs Past Simple

Four activities to help you choose the right tense — using context from the trail, races, and riding experiences.

Present Perfect
  • No specific time given
  • Life experience (ever / never)
  • Recent past with present result
  • With: just, already, yet, ever, never, since, for
"She has crashed three times this season."
Past Simple
  • Specific finished time stated
  • Completed event in the past
  • Sequence of past events
  • With: yesterday, last week, in 2019, ago, at 3pm
"She crashed on the final berm yesterday."
