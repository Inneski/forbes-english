How to describe a picture in English

  • Begin with a general overview: "The picture shows…" or "In this image we can see…"
  • Move from the foreground (what is nearest) to the background (what is furthest away).
  • Describe the atmosphere and mood — is there a sense of foreboding, peace, energy?
  • Use precise adjectives: not just "old building" but "ruined" or "crumbling".
  • Comment on spatial relationships: "in the foreground," "to the left," "in the distance."

Model description
The foreground is dominated by rugged, uneven ground — shards of old masonry half-swallowed by damp earth and coarse grass. Through a wide aperture in the ruined wall to the left, the eye is drawn straight outdoors, where a scenic valley spreads toward a line of distant hills. To the right, a tall inhabited building rises above the plain; its elaborate fenestration — four neat rows of tall, narrow windows — catches what little light the sky offers. The clouds press low and the whole scene carries a quality of quiet foreboding, as though the landscape is holding its breath before something changes.
