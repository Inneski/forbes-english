I
Grammar
Past Tenses — Past Simple & Past Continuous
01 🎬 film set

The director _______ the scene again after lunch.

reshot
did reshoot
has reshot
was reshot
Use the simple past for completed actions. Did only appears in questions and negatives — never in affirmative sentences.
02 📸 milan market

She _______ the fabric at the market in Milan last Monday.

buyed
did buy
bought
has bought
Buy → bought. Irregular verb — never buyed. Others: go → went, take → took, make → made.
03 🎨 costume design

“I designed the whole costume _______ — nobody helped me.”

on myself
by my own
by myself
on my alone
Correct: by myself or on my own. Both mean “without help.” ✗ by my own and ✗ on myself do not exist.
04 🎬 editing suite

Which sentence is correct?

I finished the edit on my own.
I finished the edit by my own.
I finished the edit on myself.
On my own = without help. Fixed phrase. Also correct: by myself. Never by my own.
05 🎨 workshop

“I didn’t do it alone — _______ from my assistant.”
Write the past simple phrase (3 words).

I had help. Note: help is uncountable — never I had a help.
06 📸 fashion show

The stylist _______ the models when the director arrived.

dressed
was dressing
did dress
has dressed
Past continuous (was/were + -ing) = action in progress when interrupted. Signal word: when.

II
Vocabulary
Prepositions & Word Choices
07 📷 photography

She is the official photographer _______ Oasis.

from
of
by
at
Use of for association: the photographer of Oasis. From = where someone comes from.
08 🎨 costume

I was responsible for _______ costume design.

the part of the
a part from
parts in
the parts for
The part of the costume design — use the before both nouns because both are specific.
09 📸 fitting room

The model tries the jacket on. It is too small. You say: “Sorry, _______.”

it not fits
it fits not
it doesn’t fit
it don’t fits
Negation: doesn’t + base verb. ✗ fits not / ✗ not fits are impossible in English.
10 📷 berlin shoot

_______ photos from the shoot in Berlin were incredible.

That
Those
This
Them
Those = plural + not here. These = plural + here. That/This = singular. Them is a pronoun, not a determiner.
11 🎬 backstage

“The backstage area was completely _______.” (Deutsch: chaotisch)

Chaotic /keɪ‘ɔtɪk/. German -isch → English -ic: fantastisch → fantastic, dramatisch → dramatic.

III
Grammar
Question Forms
Structure

Q phrase + auxiliary + subject + main verb/complement
Where were you yesterday?  ·  What should I ask you?  ·  What are you doing tomorrow?

12 📸 presentation

Put the words in the correct order:
you / presenting / are / what / tomorrow

What are you presenting tomorrow? — Q word + aux (are) + subject (you) + verb-ing.
13 🎬 storyboard

Someone took your storyboard. Which question is correct?

Who stole my storyboard?
Who did stole my storyboard?
Who my storyboard stole?
When who is the subject, no auxiliary needed: Who stole…? Compare: Who did you call? (who = object).
14 🎨 workshop

Make this a yes/no question: “You were at the workshop yesterday.”

Were you at the workshop yesterday?
Where were you at the workshop yesterday?
You were at the workshop yesterday?
Yes/No question with to be: invert → Were you…? No extra auxiliary needed.
15 🎬 past continuous

Complete: “_______ was Peter doing at 9pm yesterday?”

What was Peter doing at 9pm yesterday? — What + was + subject + verb-ing.

IV
Error Correction
Spot the Mistake
16 📸 grammar

Find the mistake in: “Where does Mary lives?”

lives → live
does → is
No mistake
After does, always use the base form: Where does Mary live? The –s moves to does, not the main verb.
17 🎬 oasis

Which sentence contains an error?

She is the photographer from Oasis.
She is the photographer of Oasis.
She works as a photographer for Oasis.
From = origin/location. Use of or for to show professional association with a band or group.
18 📸 fitting

Choose the one correct sentence:

It doesn’t fit.
It fits not.
It not fits.
English negatives: do/does + not + base verb. The verb never carries negation directly. ✓ It doesn’t fit.
19 🎨 artwork

You present your collection. Which is correct?

I made this collection on my own.
I made this collection by my own.
I made this collection on myself.
✓ on my own / ✓ by myself — both correct. ✗ by my own and ✗ on myself do not exist.

V
Phonetics
Pronunciation Focus
20

stomach

Which stress pattern is correct?

STUM-uk
sto-MACH
STO-mak
/ˈstʌmək/ — the o sounds like a short u, the ch is silent. Stress on first syllable: STUM-uk.
21

here

Which word does it rhyme with?

ear
hair
her
Here /hɪər/ rhymes with ear, near, fear, year. Not hair /hɛər/ or her /hɛː/.
22

Pearson

How is the first syllable pronounced?

PEER-son
PAR-son
PER-son
Pearson /ˈpɪərsən/ — sounds like “PEER-son.” Different from person /ˈpɛːsən/!
23

8th

How do you say “the 8th of March”?

the eighth of March
the eightieth of March
the eight of March
8th = eighth /eɪtθ/. The th ending is essential. Say “the eighth of March” or “March the eighth.”
