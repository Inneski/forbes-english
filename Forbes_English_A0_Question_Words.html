Question Words

Fragewörter · mit deutscher Unterstützung

In English, many questions begin with a question word. These words ask for specific information: a person, a thing, a place, a time, a reason, or a way something is done.

A simple pattern for beginners: question word + verb + subject?

Example: Where do you live?  |  How are you?
