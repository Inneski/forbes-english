Before anything else

A pitch is an argument, not a description

1 · The tension

Every good narrative starts from two things that pull against each other — public access and private contemplation, weight and lightness.

Without a tension there is nothing for the building to resolve, and nothing for you to say.

2 · The decision

Name the convention, then depart from it: rather than defaulting to a corridor plan…

Departing from a norm you have not named reads as ignorance. Naming it reads as judgement.

3 · The translation

Turn the abstract idea into something the client can picture: its edges blur into the terrain.

If a non-specialist cannot repeat your idea back, you have described the drawing, not the concept.

🗣Roleplay · in threes

One designer, two clients. The clients must interrupt at least twice.

  • Open on the tension your concept resolves. Two sentences, no specification.
  • A client says it does not fit their brand. Respond without defending the work.
  • A client cuts the budget by a fifth. Reframe it in front of them.
  • Close by naming what this is a direction for, not a solution to.
