Section 3 · 5 points
True or False?
Is each grammar claim about these Minecraft sentences correct? Click T or F — then read the explanation.
Statement 1
In "Villagers trade using emeralds as currency", the Present Simple is used because trading is a permanent mechanic — a general truth about the game.
True. Present Simple describes permanent facts, rules, and states — including fixed game mechanics. "Villagers trade using emeralds" is a defining truth about how Minecraft functions.
Statement 2
"She has been crafting a beacon since she found the first Wither skull" — this sentence correctly uses Present Perfect Continuous to show an activity that started in the past and is still ongoing.
True. "Has been crafting" = Present Perfect Continuous. The activity (crafting/gathering materials for a beacon) started at a past point (finding the first skull) and is still in progress now.
Statement 3
"He was mining diamonds when suddenly a Creeper was exploding next to him" — both underlined verbs are correctly in the Past Continuous.
False. "Was exploding" is wrong. A Creeper explosion is a single, sudden, completed event — it should be Past Simple: "a Creeper exploded." The Past Continuous is correct for the ongoing background action (mining), not the interrupting event.
Statement 4
"By the end of Update Aquatic, Mojang had already introduced dolphins, shipwrecks, and buried treasure" — the Past Perfect here correctly describes actions completed before a specific past point.
True. "Had already introduced" = Past Perfect Simple. These features were added before (or by the end of) a specific past moment (the release of Update Aquatic) — exactly the use case for Past Perfect.
Statement 5
"I have visited the End last year and defeated the dragon on my first attempt" is grammatically correct because the speaker is describing an experience with present relevance.
False. "Last year" is a specific past time expression — incompatible with the Present Perfect. The sentence must use Past Simple: "I visited the End last year and defeated the dragon." Present Perfect cannot be combined with finished time references.
