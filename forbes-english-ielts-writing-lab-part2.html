The Five Essay Types

SPOT THE TYPE BEFORE YOU PLAN — IT DECIDES YOUR WHOLE STRUCTURE

1 · Opinion

Agree, disagree, or partly agree — and defend one clear position throughout.

2 · Discussion

Discuss both sides fairly, then give your own opinion — usually in the conclusion.

3 · Advantage / Disadvantage

Weigh benefits against drawbacks — including "outweigh" questions.

4 · Cause / Problem / Solution

Explain why something happens and what could realistically fix it.

5 · Direct Question

Answer one, two, or three separate questions directly — don't merge them into one.

A recycled topic can resurface as a completely different type. The same subject — art funding, say — can be reworded into an Opinion, a Discussion, or a Direct Question essay. Read the instruction line every time, never just the topic.

Beyond the Basics

SCORED · FOUR FUNCTIONS THAT LIFT AN ESSAY FROM BAND 6 TO BAND 7+

Concession

Accept a point, then argue on anyway: despite, in spite of, nevertheless, even so.

Emphasis

Strengthen a point you've just made: indeed, in fact, notably.

Weighing up

Signal your overall judgement: on balance, on the whole, all things considered.

Summarising

Open the final paragraph: to summarise, in conclusion, overall.
