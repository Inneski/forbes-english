Briefing before you start

You're joining the watch for this shift. Every scenario below is built around real fire-brigade situations — evacuations, safety checks, callouts, and station life — and every one of them needs a modal verb to sound right.

  • Rules vs. advice — telling a strict regulation (must) apart from a friendly tip (should / ought to)
  • Necessity vs. choice — have to / need to against needn't and don't have to
  • Ability and possibility — can, could and might in real emergency contexts

Five activities, including a free speaking & writing round. Twenty-four scored questions. Your score is tracked the whole way through.

Bonus round · Free speaking or writing

No right or wrong answer here

Speak or write freely about the prompts below. Try to use as many modal verbs as you can: must, have to, should, can, might.

  • Describe a shift at a fire station — what does a firefighter have to do, and what shouldn't they do?
  • Talk about a rule you had to follow at work or school that you disagreed with. What should it have been instead?
  • What might happen if a fire crew doesn't follow safety procedures?
