Part 4 · Grammar — HAS or HAVE?
📋 He/she/it → always HAS. We/you/they/I → always HAVE. Never "haves"!
Q 14 / 22
The man ________ a remote control in his hand.
"The man" = he → he/she/it ALWAYS gets HAS. Never "have" or "haves"!
Q 15 / 22
Eleven ________ special telekinetic powers that can move objects with her mind.
Eleven = she → she HAS!
Q 16 / 22
Mike, Dustin and Lucas ________ a map of the tunnels hidden under Hawkins.
Three people = they → they/we/you/I always get HAVE!
Part 5 · Grammar — NOR (nicht OR!)
🚫 After a negative statement (doesn't, not, never), we use NOR — not OR! "Neither… NOR" always go together.
Q 17 / 22
She doesn't watch television, ________ does she listen to the radio.
After "doesn't / not / never" → we use NOR, not OR!
Q 18 / 22
Will is neither in Hawkins ________ in the Upside Down. Nobody knows where he is!
Neither… NOR always go together as a pair!
Part 6 · Grammar — Present Simple vs Continuous
⚡ STATE verbs (look, seem, know, like) → Present Simple. ACTION verbs happening RIGHT NOW → Present Continuous (be + -ing).
Q 19 / 22
The government agents ________ very serious when they arrive at the lab. (→ state verb: how they appear)
"Look" here = how they appear = state verb → Present Simple: "They look"!
Q 20 / 22
The two agents are meeting right now. They ________ — it's happening at this very moment! (→ action verb)
Happening RIGHT NOW → be + verb-ing (Present Continuous)!
Q 21 / 22
The kids look at the forest. "________ a lot of trees here," says Mike, "we'll never find him!"
Trees = plural → There ARE (not There IS)!
