✅ POSITIVE — Afirmativo
Subject + Past Verb
⚠️ Regular verbs add -ed (waited, enjoyed). Irregular verbs change completely (go → went, see → saw, ride → rode). (Los verbos irregulares cambian de forma.)
❌ NEGATIVE — Negativo
Subject + didn't + Base Verb
⚠️ The main verb returns to its base form — DID does all the past work! (El verbo principal vuelve a su forma base. ¡DID hace el trabajo!)
❓ QUESTIONS — Preguntas
Did + Subject + Base Verb + ?
💬 Short answers: Yes, I did.  /  No, I didn't. (Así se responde de forma corta.)

🗂️Quick Summary — Resumen Rápido

✅ Positive

Subject + past verb
I rode SheiKra.
She saw the cheetahs.
We ate near the Serengeti.
Monté / Ella vio / Comimos

❌ Negative

Subject + didn't + base verb
I didn't ride SheiKra.
She didn't see the lions.
We didn't wait long.
No monté / No vio / No esperamos

❓ Question

Did + subject + base verb?
Did you ride Montu?
Did she see the giraffes?
Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
¿Montaste? / ¿Vio? / Sí / No
