The Grammar Forecast

A forecast is a guess with a number on it. English has a word for every number: is bound to when you are certain, might when it could go either way, won't when you would bet against it. On this chart the weather in each sea area shows how sure you are. Read the sky, then pick the words.

Reading the forecast

How sure are you?

These are all ways of talking about the future, or about something you cannot see. The percentages are rough; what matters is the order.

Bound to, sure to: almost certain

Use them when you have good reasons. They often sound like a warning or a joke.

The ferry is bound to be late in this wind.

Will and won't: a confident forecast

The plain prediction: you are sure, but you are not promising.

It will rain tonight.

Probably: after will, before won't

The word order is the commonest mistake on this chart: will probably, but probably won't.

She'll probably call later, but she probably won't come.

Likely is an adjective

It follows be: is likely to, is unlikely to. Likely on its own does not replace probably.

The wind is unlikely to drop before Friday.

May, might, could: either way

All three mean about 50%. Might is the most common in speech.

We might sail tomorrow.

Definitely makes it 100% or 0%

Definitely and certainly go in the same place as probably: after will, before won't.

It definitely won't snow in July.

The shipping forecast

Three ways to say likely

A weather forecaster rarely says maybe. These patterns sound more precise, and they turn up in reports, news and exam essays.

Likely, chance, possible

Each pattern has its own shape. Learn the shape with the word.

PatternExample
something is likely to + verbRain is likely to reach the coast by evening.
It is likely that + clauseIt is likely that the ferry will be cancelled.
There is a good chance of + nounThere is a good chance of fog.
There is a good chance (that) + clauseThere is a good chance we will be late.
It is possible that + clauseIt is possible that the storm will turn north.

There's a good chance of rain this afternoon.

Not only the weather
