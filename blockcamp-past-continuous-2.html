What it means

One long, one short

The one that was already running

It started before, and it did not stop for you.

I was driving homethe dogs were sleepingESyo iba conduciendo a casa · los perros dormíanDEich fuhr gerade nach Hause · die Hunde schliefen

The one that cut across it

Short, finished, and it happened in the middle.

the call camethe creeper explodedESllegó la llamada · el creeper explotóDEder Anruf kam · der Creeper explodierte

The join

‘while’ and ‘when’

‘while’ + the long one
ESmientras + la acción largaDEwährend + die lange Handlung
longWhile I was cooking
longAs we were digging
‘when’ + the short one
EScuando + la acción cortaDEals + die kurze Handlung
shortwhen the phone rang
shortwhen the roof fell in
The same night, both ways
ESsonaba el teléfono mientras yo cocinabaDEdas Telefon klingelte, während ich kochte
one wayWhile I was cooking, the phone rang.
the otherWhen the phone rang, I was cooking.

‘WHILE’ TAKES THE LONG ONE — ‘WHEN’ TAKES THE SHORT ONE!

What else it does

Three more jobs

Two at once
ESella cocinaba mientras él deshacía las maletasDEsie kochte, während er auspackte
both longShe was cooking while he was unpacking.
A stretch that ended
ESverdad durante un tiempoDEeine Zeit lang wahr
true for a whileThat year we were living above the bakery.
The complaint
ESsiempre estaba perdiendo las llavesDEer verlor ständig seine Schlüssel
annoyedHe was always losing his keys.

always / forever / constantly + VERB-ING = annoyed

The exception

The verbs that refuse the -ing

Thinking
ESno ’was knowing’DEnicht ’was knowing’
know, believeI knew the answer.
rememberShe remembered the way.
Feeling and senses
ESno ’was wanting’DEnicht ’was wanting’
want, needShe wanted to leave.
see, hear, seemIt seemed quiet.
Having
ESno ’was having’DEnicht ’was having’
own, belongWe had two horses then.
have = possessThe house belonged to Alex.

a state is not an activity — use the PAST SIMPLE

Three mistakes

Worth naming, so you can dodge them

Not this
ESlos tres fallos más comunesDEdie drei häufigsten Fehler
1They was waiting.
2I was knowing him.
3While I was walking, it was starting to rain.
This
ESestaban esperando · lo conocía · empezó a lloverDEsie warteten · ich kannte ihn · es fing an zu regnen
1They were waiting.
2I knew him.
3While I was walking, it started to rain.
How long is each one?

the top three take WAS / WERE + -ING, the bottom two take the PAST SIMPLE

The fork

Inside it, or looking back at it

Past continuous

You are standing in the middle of it.

I was reading when the lights went outESestaba leyendo cuando se fue la luzDEich las gerade, als das Licht ausging

Past simple

You are looking back at the whole finished thing.

I read it, and then I sleptESlo leí y luego dormíDEich las es und dann schlief ich
