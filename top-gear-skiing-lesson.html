03

Grammar Engine

HOW THE PAST SIMPLE WORKS

Formation

✅ POSITIVE
Subject + verb-ed / irregular form
Jeremy crashed the car into a snowbank.
Richard went off-piste immediately.
❌ NEGATIVE
Subject + didn't + base verb
James didn't fall over — not once.
They didn't find the resort easy.
❓ QUESTION
Did + subject + base verb?
Did they race down the mountain?
What did Jeremy break this time?
🕒 TIME SIGNALS
yesterday · last week · in 2003
ago · at that moment · when · then
⚠ Irregular verbs don't follow the -ed rule. Common ones in this lesson: go → went · drive → drove · fall → fell · crash → crashed* · break → broke · win → won · buy → bought · see → saw · hit → hit
*crash is regular!

05

Reading: The Race

READ AND NOTICE THE HIGHLIGHTED PAST SIMPLE VERBS

TOP GEAR · ALPINE SPECIAL

Last winter, the Top Gear team travelled to the French Alps for a special challenge. The producers gave each presenter a sports car and a pair of skis. The challenge was simple: race from the top of the mountain to the village below — the skier against the car.

Jeremy chose to drive the car. He put his foot on the accelerator and went immediately off the road. He hit a snow barrier and broke the front bumper. The camera crew laughed and filmed everything.

Richard decided to ski. He strapped on his skis, looked down the enormous slope, and said nothing. Then he pushed off and shot down the mountain at incredible speed. He fell twice but didn't stop.

James didn't race at all. He went to a café, ordered a hot chocolate, and watched the whole disaster from a warm terrace. In the end, Richard won. Jeremy arrived last — with no bumper and a bruised ego.
