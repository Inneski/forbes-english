Density, and what it costs

Nominalisation: turning a claim into a thing

the automation of thought

A whole clause — people stop thinking for themselves — packed into a noun phrase.

Once it is a noun, it can be the subject of your next sentence.

cognitive offloading

Naming a mechanism so that it can be measured, cited and argued with: knowledge collapse, model collapse.

Naming the thing is the first move in owning the argument about it.

individual gain, collective loss

The essay’s whole thesis in four words, because both halves are nouns.

A nominalised pair is what makes a claim quotable.

the price

The elimination of interaction-dense work has nobody eliminating anything.

Same tell as the passive: ask who did it, and see whether the sentence can answer.

Seven verbs, one scale

The vocabulary of decline is not a synonym set

thin · thin out

Fewer of them, less densely: junior roles thin out, the substrate is allowed to thin.

About how many there are, never about quality.

narrow · converge

The range shrinks; the things inside it become alike: collectively, they had converged.

Converge is about similarity and can never mean fewer.

erode · attenuate

Worn away from outside; weakened as a signal: a slow attenuation of the substrate.

Erode takes surfaces and institutions; attenuate takes signals and effects.

degrade · collapse

Quality falls; the structure goes entirely: models collapse when trained on their own output.

Collapse is the end state. The other six are the road to it.

Reporting, or agreeing

The verb you choose is a verdict

neutral

argues · writes · observes · reports · frames it as

You have reported the claim. You have not signed it.

endorsing

establishes · demonstrates · shows · quantifies

Use one of these and you have agreed. That is a decision, not a style.

limiting

assumes · concedes · declines to examine · comes closest to acknowledging

These name what the source did not do — the sharpest tool in a critique.

distancing

reaches for a phrase · is typically framed as · I suspect mere rhetorical flourish

You describe the move rather than accepting the fact.

The shape of an argument

Concede properly, then turn once

concede first

Yes, scaling matters, architecture matters, and compute matters.

Give the strongest version of the other side. A weak concession reads as a trick.

pivot once

But none of these will continue to deliver if the social substrate is allowed to thin.

One sentence, and it carries the essay. Two pivots and the reader stops believing either.

the false agreement

So far, so on message. But when the researchers examined the full body of stories…

Set the consensus up in its own words, then break it with the evidence.

correct the cause

It is smarter not because the architecture changed but because the civilization did.

You accept the fact and reassign its reason. Far harder to answer than a flat denial.

Premises and payoff

Conditional chains, and the inverted form

if … and if … then

If capability depends on social complexity — and if deployment reduces it — then the technology undermines itself.

Two premises, one conclusion. The dashes hold the second premise open.

inversion

Were the substrate to thin further… · Should the springs dry up… · Had the tails survived…

Formal, and it front-loads the condition. No if, and never if … would.

the thought experiment

Suppose you could travel to Egypt in 3000 BC… The result would be a system capable of…

Suppose sets it up and would runs to the end of it, without ever slipping into will.

where the hedge goes

Their compound effect may be catastrophic — hedged conclusion, unhedged premises.

Hedge the conclusion. Hedge the premises too and you have argued nothing.

Figurative language that argues

A live metaphor makes a claim you can dispute

substrate

What a thing grows on: the social substrate, the springs feeding the reservoir.

It chooses the frame: intelligence as a crop, not as a machine.

inheritance

Inheritances, if consumed without reinvestment, eventually run out.

An extended metaphor has to stay inside one frame: consume, reinvest, run out.

borrowed precision

a tragedy of the commons · the tails of the distribution · a fossil of social interaction

Taken from a field that defines it. It must survive being read literally.

live or dead

a doom-spiral is nearly dead; the springs feeding it are drying up is alive.

A live metaphor advances the argument. A dead one is just a word you reached for.
