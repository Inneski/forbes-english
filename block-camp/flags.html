YOUR LOOKOUT

Every flag you plant raises your lookout tower. Score 50% on a camp’s Part 1 to raise its storey, or on a station to light its lamp. Score 75% and that piece goes gold; pass a Part 2 and its flag carries a star. Build all eighteen and the beacon fires.

THE PARTS LIST

Every flag is a piece of the Lookout: a camp on the climb raises a storey, a station on the descent hangs a lamp.

  • Not yet · under 50%
  • Planted · 50%: the piece is built
  • Gold · 75%: the piece goes gold
  • Star · 50% on Part 2
