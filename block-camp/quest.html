THE CLIMB

Nine camps, one tense each, two parts to a camp. Read a part to its last slide and the pip fills; fill both and the camp is pitched. The adventures that teach a camp's tense hang off its stop.

THE DESCENT

Down the far side: every camp again in the passive, and the Trial at the bottom with every tense and no labels.

THE ADVENTURES

Branching stories with a grammar point in the plot. Your best score, the tiles you have found and every ending you have reached are kept here.
