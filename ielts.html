Academic Module · C1

IELTS Academic

Four skills and the vocabulary that feeds them. Each route is ordered — start at the top of one and work down, rather than picking lessons at random, because the later ones assume the earlier ones.

Every lesson is click-through, scored where scoring teaches something, and ends with language you actually produce rather than recognise.

This is the Academic module. Speaking and Listening are identical on General Training, and Reading uses the same question types — but GT Task 1 is a letter, not a report, so the Task 1 lessons will not match a GT paper.
