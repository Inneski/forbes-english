IELTS Academic Writing

Nine lessons, in order. Start with the Report. Task 1 comes first here because it comes first on the paper — but Task 2 carries twice the marks, so that is where the hours should go. The numbering is teaching order, which is why the model answers (Part 4) sit before the timed practice (Part 3).

Every lesson is click-through, scored where scoring teaches something, and ends with writing you actually produce rather than recognise.
