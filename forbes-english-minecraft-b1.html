Surviving the First Night

The tenses a player needs to tell the story, and the words the game gives them

B1 · Intermediate Tense choice & game vocabulary 20 slides
Before the questions

Three tenses, three jobs

What you always do

The present simple carries habits and rules: when I find diamonds, I always make armour. It is the tense for what is true every time, not what is happening now.

Every morning I am checking my chest is the classic B1 slip. A routine is not in progress.

What has just happened

The present perfect links a finished action to now: I have just found a village. Just means a moment ago, already means sooner than expected, yet means still not.

Add yesterday and it breaks — a finished time needs the past simple.

How long it has been going on

I have been playing for two hours. The activity is still running and the sentence measures it. For takes a length of time; since takes a starting point.

For two hours, since Tuesday. Ask whether the word names a stretch or a moment.

The pairings

The word the game actually uses

Verbs that only take one noun

You sleep in a bed, not rest in one. You take damage, never make it. You carry a torch, you do not wear it. Each pairing is fixed and has to be learned whole.

Make damage is the commonest of these errors, and it is not English.

Words that describe how often

Diamonds are rare, or uncommon — that is about how seldom they appear. Difficult and hard are about the effort of getting them, which is a different claim.

Rare things can be easy to get once found. The two words are not interchangeable.

The connector that warns

Otherwise introduces what happens if you do not: build a shelter, otherwise mobs will attack. Or and or else do the same job in a lower register.

It always points forward to a consequence, and the consequence is always the bad one.

The vocabulary

Game words that are ordinary English underneath

Spawn and respawn

To spawn is to appear in the world — the first time, or after dying. The re- in respawn means again, exactly as it does in rebuild and retry.

Your spawn point is where you reappear: your bed, or where the world started you.

Craft, mine, smelt

To craft is to make something with skill from materials. To mine is to dig for stone, coal or iron. To smelt is to heat raw ore in a furnace until it becomes usable metal.

All three are real English outside the game, and all three mean the same thing there.

Mob, biome, inventory

A mob is any moving creature, friendly or not. A biome is a region with its own weather, plants and landscape. Your inventory is everything you are carrying.

Biome and inventory are used unchanged in geography and in business.

🗣Roleplay · in pairs

One of you has played for years, the other has never opened the game. Three minutes each, then swap.

  • Explain the first ten minutes of a new world to someone who has never played anything.
  • Say what you have been doing in a game recently and what you have just finished.
  • Describe your routine in a game you play — what you always do, and in what order.
  • Warn your partner about three dangers, using otherwise each time.
✍️Writing · 120–150 words

Write the survival guide you would give a friend on their first night. Say what they must do and in what order, what happens otherwise, and what you have learned from playing. Use the present simple for the rules and the present perfect for your own experience.
