How to Use This Pack

FOR THE MARKING TEACHER — NOT AUTO-GRADED, BY DESIGN

Each studio produces a plain-text file with the student's plan and their finished writing, ready to open and mark by hand. Nothing here is auto-scored — this page is a quick reference for marking, not a marking engine.

Task Achievement / Response

Does the answer do what the prompt actually asked? For Task 1: is there a clear overview, and are the right features selected? For Task 2: is the exact question answered, with ideas developed and supported rather than just listed?

Coherence & Cohesion

Is the writing organised into clear paragraphs, each built around one main idea? Do linking words connect ideas naturally, without feeling forced or repeated too often?

Lexical Resource

Is the vocabulary varied and precise for the topic? Look for genuine paraphrasing of the prompt rather than its exact words repeated back.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy

Is there a mix of simple and complex sentences? How frequent are the grammar errors, and do any of them get in the way of understanding?
