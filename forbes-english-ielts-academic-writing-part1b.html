Golden rules for Task 2

Task 2 counts twice as much as Task 1 — give it 40 minutes

Answer the exact question. The biggest mistake: answering a different question to the one asked. "Advantages and disadvantages" ≠ "Do advantages outweigh disadvantages?"

Quality over quantity. Don't brainstorm a long list of weak ideas. Choose one or two ideas per paragraph and explain them deeply, with an example.

Golden rule three

Plan before you write

Spend the first 5–10 minutes planning: topic, question type, position, two ideas with examples.

Linking Words Bank

Adding ideas
in addition — In addition, remote work reduces commuting costs.
furthermore — Furthermore, employees report higher job satisfaction.
moreover — Moreover, companies save on office space.
what is more — What is more, it widens the talent pool.

Contrasting
however — However, isolation can become a real problem.
on the other hand — On the other hand, office work builds stronger teams.
whereas — Younger workers adapt easily, whereas older staff often struggle.
although / even though — Although costs fall, productivity can suffer.

Reference · not scored

Linking Words Bank

Cause & effect
as a result — As a result, city-centre traffic has fallen.
consequently — Consequently, air quality has improved.
this means that — This means that fewer parking spaces are needed.
due to — Due to rising fuel costs, car use has declined.

Sequencing
firstly / secondly / finally — Firstly, consider the cost; secondly, the time saved.
subsequently — Subsequently, more firms adopted the same policy.
meanwhile — Meanwhile, smaller towns saw little change.

🗣Discussion · in pairs

Plan aloud first. Two ideas only, each with an example.

  • Say your position in one sentence. Then defend it for sixty seconds without repeating yourself.
  • Give your partner one idea and one example. They tell you whether the example actually proves the idea.
  • Take the opposite position to the one you hold, and argue it well enough to be believed.
