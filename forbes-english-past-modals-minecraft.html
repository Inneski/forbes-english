Quick Grammar Guide: Past Modals

Form Meaning & Use
should have + past participle Advice / criticism about the past — "It was a mistake not to do it"
could have + past participle Past ability / missed opportunity — "It was possible but didn't happen"
must have + past participle Logical deduction about the past — "I'm almost certain this happened"
might have + past participle Possibility in the past — "Perhaps this happened, I'm not sure"
needn't have + past participle Unnecessary action — "You did it, but it wasn't necessary"
