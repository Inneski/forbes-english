Speaking & Writing

Now say it yourself

You have read ten accounts of how a strange little show was made, cancelled, missed and finally brought back. Recognising the language is half of it — here is where you use it.

Discussion · in pairs

  • A network is about to cancel a show with a small but devoted audience. One of you argues for keeping it; the other argues for cutting it. Use would have, should have and might have.
  • Describe something you loved that was ended before its time — a series, a band, a place, a job. What was lost when it went?
  • Twin Peaks came back twenty-five years later. Is it ever a good idea to return to something that finished? Give a reason your partner has not thought of.
  • Report what your partner said, without their words: He said that…, She claimed that…, They admitted that…

Writing · 180–220 words

Write a short review of a series, film or book that divided its audience. Explain what it was trying to do, why some people rejected it, and whether it has lasted.
