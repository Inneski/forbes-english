Reading — Food & Culture in Ecuador

Read the passage about Ecuadorian food and culture. Hover over highlighted words for their meanings. Then decide whether each statement is True or False.

Ecuador's food culture is as diverse as its landscapes. In the coastal region, fresh seafood dominates the menu. Ceviche — raw fish marinated in lemon or lime juice — is perhaps the most beloved dish. Along the coast, shrimp and tilapia are harvested from farms and rivers alike.

In the Andes highlands, the diet is heartier. Locro de papa is a rich potato soup often served with avocado and cheese. The indigenous Quechua people have cultivated potatoes in this region for thousands of years — long before the vegetable spread to Europe.

Ecuador is the world's largest exporter of bananas. It also produces some of the finest cacao in the world — the raw ingredient in chocolate. Ecuadorian "Nacional" cacao is considered a premium variety, prized by chocolate-makers globally.

The country's festivals are equally colourful. Inti Raymi, the Festival of the Sun, is one of the most important indigenous celebrations in the Andes. Participants wear traditional costumes and dance for days to honour Inti, the sun god of the Inca people.

Grammar — The Passive Voice

The passive voice is used when the action is more important than who does it. It is very common in written English — especially in reports, news, and descriptions of places. Study the patterns and complete the exercises.

How to form the passive voice
Present: is/are + past participle
Bananas are exported from Ecuador every day.
Past: was/were + past participle
The Galápagos Islands were visited by Darwin in 1835.
Present perfect: has/have been + pp
The rainforest has been protected by law since 2008.
Modal: can/must/should be + pp
Endangered species must be protected by the government.

Rewrite using the passive voice. Fill in the correct form.

1. People speak Spanish in Ecuador. → Spanish in Ecuador.
2. Farmers grow cacao in the coastal region. → Cacao in the coastal region.
3. Darwin discovered many species on the islands. → Many species by Darwin.
4. The government has declared many areas protected. → Many areas protected.
5. Tourists must not feed the animals. → The animals by tourists.
Writing tip: In formal or academic writing, the passive voice is preferred because it sounds more objective. For example, "It is believed that Ecuador has over 1,600 bird species" sounds more formal than "People believe Ecuador has..."
