# KRAKEN: THE BLACK TIDE — the image list for Midjourney

Fifty-nine plates, numbered in play order to match the **Picture** field in
`STORY.md`. One picture per scene, plus a cover per part and one closing
plate. Endings reuse their part's cover unless a plate is listed.

Deliver as 3:2 (`--ar 3:2`). They will be resized to 1536 × 1024 WebP under
200 KB by the pipeline; generate at whatever size Midjourney gives you.

## The style contract — paste this sentence, verbatim, at the start of every prompt

> Painted digital illustration in the manner of a Scottish oil sketch: heavy
> wet brushwork, muted North Atlantic palette of slate, pewter and bottle
> green with one warm sodium-amber light in every frame, rain-dark stone,
> rust and chipped yellow paint, soft edges, no black outlines, eye-level
> camera set slightly low as if from a boat.

Written below as **[STYLE]**. It never varies. Two plates lit differently
read as two games.

## Three rules that cannot bend

1. **No lettering anywhere.** No boat names on hulls, no shop signs, no
   chart labels, no sail numbers, no newspaper, no writing on the log or
   the chequebook. Image models garble English and a broken word on a
   language lesson reads as a mistake in the lesson. Every clue names a
   physical object, never a written one. Add `--no text, letters, writing,
   signage, watermark` to every prompt.
2. **The glowing object sits right of centre, between 60% and 95% of the
   width, and the left half of the frame stays quiet** — open water, fog, a
   wall, sky, an empty pier. The text panel sits on the left and covers up to
   54% of the frame. Nothing that must be seen lives left of the middle.
3. **Dark, but never black.** Half of this story is at night, at dusk or in
   a cave. Every such plate needs its one amber light source *in frame* on or
   near the object, because the glow marker takes its colour from the object
   under it. No crushed blacks, no heavy grain, no lens flare.

## The character sheet — paste the line for anyone in the frame

- **Isla Munro:** mid-thirties, dark red hair tied back, navy police
  waterproof with the collar up, pale, no hat.
- **Angus Tulloch:** fifties but weathered to seventy, grey-white beard,
  oiled bottle-green wool jumper, flat cap, a long pale scar down the right
  forearm.
- **Dr Rhona Vass:** late twenties, black curly hair, round wire glasses,
  mustard-yellow fleece under an orange flotation vest.
- **Provost Menzies:** sixties, heavy grey overcoat, thin grey hair, red
  rosette on the lapel.
- **Fergus Brannan:** fifties, yellow oilskin jacket, grey stubble, a
  handheld VHF on a lanyard.
- **Fiona Sinclair:** forties, fair hair soaked flat by rain, green raincoat.
- **Ewan Sinclair:** fifteen, black wetsuit, red sit-on-top kayak.
- **Kirsty Munro:** twelve, orange buoyancy aid, white sailing dinghy.
- **Sandy Weir:** sixties, red marshal's bib with no writing, grey inflatable.
- **The Docherty brothers:** two big men in matching black drysuits, an
  orange RIB.
- **The *Kittiwake*:** a twelve-metre wooden creel boat, dark green hull,
  white wheelhouse, old tyres along the gunwale, four yellow marker barrels
  lashed to the rail.
- **The kraken:** never shown whole. A black arm with a rim of wet light
  along it, a pale mantle under green water, a wake with nothing in front of
  it. No eye until plate 53. No full body on any plate.
