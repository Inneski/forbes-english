Water Polo Vocabulary

Wasserball-Wortschatz · mit deutscher Unterstützung

Water polo is a team sport played in a swimming pool. Two teams try to throw the ball into the other team's goal.

In this lesson you will learn key water polo words in English, with their German meaning.
