Full-Time Pressure

Ninety minutes, eleven decisions — the English of a dressing room under strain

C1 Advanced Role-play 11 scenes
How this works

You are the player. Every reply counts.

Each scene puts a teammate, a referee or a reporter in front of you. Pick the reply that a fluent speaker would actually use — tone matters as much as grammar.

Three of the four options are wrong for a reason: too blunt, too formal, too passive, or escalating when you needed to defuse. Every one tells you which.

Watch the team spirit bar. It rises when you handle a moment well and falls when you do not — the same way a dressing room does.

1 · Tunnel — Pre-Match
Team spirit

It's ten minutes before kick-off. Your captain notices a young teammate looking visibly nervous ahead of his first big start.

Teammate (quietly)"I don't know if I'm ready for this. What if I mess it up?"

How do you respond?

2 · 23' — Contentious Decision
Team spirit

The referee waves away a clear penalty appeal after you were caught in the box. Your captain jogs over as tempers start to rise around you.

Captain"Keep it together — arguing won't change his mind, and we can't afford to lose our heads here."

You need to calm down a furious teammate who's about to confront the referee. What do you say?

3 · 38' — Teammate's Error
Team spirit

A misplaced pass from your midfielder leads directly to the opposition's opening goal. He's clearly furious with himself as play restarts.

Midfielder"That's on me. I've cost us the goal."

How do you respond as you jog past him?

4 · 44' — Injury on the Pitch
Team spirit

An opponent goes down clutching his ankle after a strong but fair challenge from you. The physio is on, and the crowd is jeering.

Opposition player (on the ground)"That was reckless — you could've broken my leg!"

You crouch down to check on him. What do you say?

5 · Half-Time — Dressing Room
Team spirit

1-0 down at the break. The manager wants tactical feedback before addressing the squad.

Manager"What are you seeing out there? Be honest with me."

You give your honest tactical assessment. What do you say?

6 · Half-Time — System Change
Team spirit

Still 1-0 down. The manager is weighing up a switch from a flat back four to a back three to get more numbers forward in the second half.

Manager"I'm thinking of going to a back three to get more bodies forward. What's your take?"

The manager wants your tactical opinion before he commits to the change. What do you say?

7 · 58' — Press or Drop Off
Team spirit

Since the system change, the opposition goalkeeper is now under no pressure with the ball at his feet. Your winger looks across at you for a signal on how to approach it.

Winger (shouting across)"Do we go and press him, or drop into a block?"

As the more experienced player, what instruction do you give?

8 · 68' — Substitution
Team spirit

You're being brought off for a fresh striker. You're frustrated, but the manager's decision is final.

Manager (as you reach the touchline)"Good effort out there. We need fresh legs up front now."

How do you react as you come off?

9 · 89' — Sledging from an Opponent
Team spirit

With the score level, an opposition defender starts needling you verbally, trying to provoke a reaction before a crucial free kick.

Opponent (smirking)"You've been anonymous all game — no wonder they're subbing your mates and not you."

How do you respond, staying focused on the free kick?

10 · Full-Time — Pitch-Side Interview
Team spirit

Final whistle: a hard-fought 1-1 draw. A reporter approaches you immediately for a reaction, microphone in hand.

Reporter"A point at home — are you satisfied with that result, or is it two points dropped?"

How do you respond to the cameras?

Bonus · Stoppage Time — Wonder Goal
Team spirit

96th minute. You've just smashed in a ridiculous, top-corner overhead kick to win it. You rip your shirt off before you even realise what you're doing. Cameras swarm you before you've even reached the bench — and today, humility can wait.

Reporter (breathless)"Unbelievable goal! What's the secret?"

You're in full showman mode. What's your reaction? (This scene rewards flair and confidence, not diplomacy — but there's still a line between funny arrogance and genuine disrespect.)
