The idea

These are not synonyms. They are vectors.

At C2 the test is almost never what does this word mean. It is which way does it push — and the four wrong options will usually push the other way.

Learn them in the four groups on the next slide and most questions answer themselves before you have read the options.

Every sentence in this lesson comes from the world of a fictional band called El Zar — their tours, their rows, their reviews.

The idea

Four directions, eighteen words

↓ Turn it down

attenuate · diminish · abate · curtail · mitigate · alleviate · assuage · mollify · circumscribe

↑ Turn it up, or set it off

exacerbate · precipitate · engender · bolster

◑ Clear it, or cloud it

elucidate · obfuscate

⌈ Back it up

corroborate · substantiate · underscore

Activation

Write the band into trouble

Use at least four: attenuate assuage precipitate obfuscate corroborate circumscribe bolster engender
🗣Discussion · in pairs

One of you manages El Zar. The other runs the label. The tour is losing money.

  • Manager: press for a bigger marketing spend without conceding the tour was mismanaged.
  • Label: curtail the tour by four dates, and mollify the manager while you do it.
  • Both: agree a public statement that does not obfuscate, yet admits nothing actionable.
