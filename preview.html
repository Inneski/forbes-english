A1 Grammar · possessives · für deutsche Schülerinnen

My bag → It’s mine.

This lesson teaches possessive adjectives and possessive pronouns together, with light German support: my book = mein Buch, but the book is mine = das Buch gehört mir.

First, learn the pattern

Deutsch: Zuerst lernen wir den Unterschied: my bag = meine Tasche, aber It’s mine = Sie gehört mir.

Do this before the quiz. The German notes are short: enough support for A1 learners, but the practice stays in English.

1. Before a noun

Use a possessive adjective.

Deutsch: Vor einem Nomen: my / your / her usw.

This is my bag. = Das ist meine Tasche.
This is your phone. = Das ist dein Handy.

Pattern: my / your / his / her / our / their + noun

2. Without a noun

Use a possessive pronoun.

Deutsch: Ohne Nomen: mine / yours / hers usw. Oft: gehört mir/dir/ihr...

The bag is mine. = Die Tasche gehört mir.
The phone is yours. = Das Handy gehört dir.

Pattern: mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs
