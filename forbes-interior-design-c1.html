Question 1 of 6
We decided to use a glass curtain wall on the south elevation to maximise the ___ light inside the space.
A
ambience light that pours in naturally
B
ambient light that fills the interior
C
ambiance light that brightens things up
D
atmosphere light that enters the room
✓ Correct: ambient light "Ambient" is the adjective form. "Ambience" and "ambiance" are nouns — they cannot modify a noun. We say "ambient light," not "ambience light."
Question 2 of 6
I ___ in this project since the initial brief, so I have a thorough understanding of the client's vision.
A
have been participating
B
was participating
C
had been participating
D
have participated
✓ Correct — both forms work here. Present perfect simple ("have participated") emphasises the connection to now — the involvement is relevant to the current situation. Present perfect continuous ("have been participating") emphasises the ongoing duration. Both are natural with "since." Either answer is marked correct.
Question 3 of 6
We've installed a storage unit ___ the counter so the kitchen feels uncluttered at eye level.
A
directly underneath of
B
just beneath of the
C
right underneath the
D
directly beneath of the
✓ Correct: right underneath the "Underneath" does not take "of" — this is a common learner error. We say "underneath the counter," never "underneath of the counter."
Question 4 of 6
A deep blue palette would make the reception area feel less ___ for visitors arriving after a long journey.
A
hospitality and friendly
B
welcoming and hospitable
C
welcoming and hospitality
D
hospitable and hospitality
✓ Correct: welcoming and hospitable "Hospitality" is a noun. After "less," we need adjectives: "welcoming" and "hospitable." Never use a noun in an adjective position.
Question 5 of 6
The pendant fitting above the dining table is a ___ light — it hangs from a long cable attached to the ceiling.
A
dropped ceiling light fixture
B
falling decorative light above
C
suspended decorative light fitting
D
hanging dropped light element
✓ Correct: suspended decorative light fitting The correct technical term is "suspended light." "Dropped" ceiling refers to a lowered ceiling structure — not a hanging light. "Suspended" is the standard professional term.
Question 6 of 6
The brand identity calls for ___ energy — the space should feel dynamic and contemporary without being aggressive.
A
youth
B
youthful
C
youngly
D
young
✓ Correct: youthful "Youthful" is the adjective that modifies "energy." "Youth" is a noun and cannot modify another noun in this context. "Youngly" does not exist. "Young energy" is technically possible but "youthful energy" is the natural, established collocation.
Activity Two
Fill in the Blank
Complete each sentence with the correct word or phrase. Pay attention to grammar and register.
Question 1 of 5
The supplier is based in Milan. It is an Italian company which high-quality wood veneers.
✓ Correct: produces Use present simple for facts about companies: "It is a company which produces…" Present continuous ("is producing") implies a temporary action, not a general, ongoing fact.
Question 2 of 5
The structure itself is already in place — the concrete floor and will anchor the whole design.
✓ Correct: already exists Use present simple for permanent, existing facts. "The concrete floor already exists" — not "is already existing," which implies a temporary or changing situation.
Question 3 of 5
The partition wall doesn't occupy much of the and also works as a decorative feature.
✓ Correct: floor space / floor area Both are natural noun phrases in a design context. "Floor space" and "floor area" are interchangeable here. Note also: no preposition after "occupy" — say "occupy much of the floor space," not "occupy much of the floor space of."
Question 4 of 5
The façade features a — a fully glazed exterior that keeps the structure light and transparent.
✓ Correct: glass curtain wall This is the standard architectural term for a non-structural outer covering of a building, typically made of glass panels. It's an important phrase in interior design and architecture presentations.
Question 5 of 5
The overall composition is — every detail has been chosen to create visual harmony.
✓ Correct: aesthetically appealing Note the spelling: "appealing" has only one 'l' — a common mistake is to write "appealling." Also remember: "aesthetically" starts with the "ee" sound /iːsˈθɛtɪks/.

Question 1 of 5
I have prepared a design drop here that outlines three possible approaches for the space.
✓ Correct: proposal "Proposal" is the noun (= a formal plan you put forward). "Propose" is the verb. In a presentation, you present a "proposal," not a "propose."
Question 2 of 5
We're balancing a modern, digital feel with an drop here warmth through the use of timber and tactile materials.
✓ Correct: analogue "Analogue" (British English) refers to a non-digital, physical quality — textures, warmth, handmade elements. It's pronounced /ˈænəlɒɡ/, with stress on the first syllable.
Question 3 of 5
The ceiling installation uses a structural drop here system to allow flexible placement of lighting and services.
✓ Correct: grid A "grid system" is a common architectural term for a modular framework. Note the pronunciation: /ɡrɪd/ — a short vowel, rhyming with "hid." Don't pronounce the vowel as a long "ee" sound.
Question 4 of 5
The brand values include drop here energy, innovation, and a sense of creative possibility.
✓ Correct: youthful "Youthful" is the adjective that modifies "energy." "Youth" is the noun — it cannot directly modify another noun in this context. Compare: "youth club" (compound noun) vs. "youthful energy" (adjective + noun).
Question 5 of 5
The overall drop here of the space draw on Scandinavian minimalism combined with warm Italian materiality.
✓ Correct: aesthetics "Aesthetics" (plural noun) refers to the visual and sensory qualities of a design. Pronunciation: /iːsˈθɛtɪks/ — starts with the "ee" sound (like "east"), then "THET-iks." This word is often mispronounced by placing the stress incorrectly.
