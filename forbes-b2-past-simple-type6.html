⚡ Grammar Reference — Past Simple

Regular verbs — add -ed

Base formPast simple
designdesigned reg
launchlaunched reg
raceraced reg
introduceintroduced reg
testtested reg
modifymodified reg
Spelling rules: verbs ending in -e → just add -d (race → raced). Verbs ending in consonant + -y → change y to -ied (modify → modified).

Irregular verbs — must memorise

Base formPast simple
becomebecame irr
drivedrove irr
winwon irr
setset irr
breakbroke irr
buildbuilt irr
Note: Some irregular verbs have no change at all — set → set. Others change vowel completely — drive → drove. There is no shortcut; these must be learnt individually.

The Honda Civic Type 6 — Past & Present

The Honda Civic has a long motorsport and road car history. The fictional "Type 6" in this lesson represents the next evolution: a high-performance variant that was designed, tested, and raced under extreme conditions. Engineers modified the chassis, introduced a new aerodynamic package, and drove it to victory.

In this lesson you will work with both regular and irregular past simple verbs — identifying them, correcting errors, completing sentences, and sequencing a race report. Pay attention to the reg and irr labels throughout.
