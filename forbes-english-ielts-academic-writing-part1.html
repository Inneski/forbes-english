Golden Rules for Task 1

MINS 0–10 · REPORT ON WHAT YOU SEE — NEVER YOUR OPINION

Time & length

Spend 20 minutes on Task 1. Write 150 words minimum, aim for 160–180 — then stop.

No opinions

Never add personal opinions, reasons, or outside knowledge. Only report what is visible.

Select, don't list

A Band 7+ answer selects and reports the main features — it does not mechanically list every number.

150–180 words · introduction → overview → body 1 → body 2

Golden Rules for Task 2

MINS 0–10 · TASK 2 IS WORTH TWICE AS MANY POINTS AS TASK 1 — LEAVE 40 MINUTES

Answer the exact question

The biggest mistake: answering a different question to the one asked. "Advantages and disadvantages" ≠ "Do advantages outweigh disadvantages?"

Quality over quantity

Don't brainstorm a long list of weak ideas. Choose one or two ideas per paragraph and explain them deeply, with an example.

Plan before you write

Spend the first 5–10 minutes planning: topic, question type, position, two ideas with examples.

250 words · introduction → body 1 → body 2 → conclusion — never a new idea in the conclusion
